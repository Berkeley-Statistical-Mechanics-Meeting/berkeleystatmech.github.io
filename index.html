Meeting Format

Invited Lecture Sessions: Thirty minute time slots for each invited lecturer, including questions.

Poster Sessions: Two separate poster sessions will be held on Friday and Saturday late afternoon. Each of these contributed presentations has a two-minute oral summary followed by a light reception with poster display and discussion. Three prizes for best poster presentations are awarded at the Sunday lunch.

Meals: Breakfast and lunch are provided on Saturday and Sunday. There will be a light reception to accompany the poster displays and discussion. For evening meals following the talks and light reception, participants are free to sample the many restaurants in and around Berkeley and San Francisco.
